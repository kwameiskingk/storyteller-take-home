STORYTELLER TAKE-HOME: DELIVERY LEAD, AI-ENABLED PRODUCTS
Candidate: Kwame Mbugua
Scenario: Aurora Basketball League, Game 3 on Friday 11 September 2026

READ IN THIS ORDER

1. brief/index.html
   The delivery brief. Open it in any browser. It has the recommendation,
   the evidence, the plan, the client update, the AI-use account and links
   to every file below. Fifteen to twenty minutes.

2. README.md
   The same content as the brief in plain text, for reading on GitHub,
   which does not render HTML.

3. ops/
   One document per person in the scenario:
   client_update_thursday_1500.md   note to Priya and the message for Jordan
   marta_friday_list.md             ranked engineering list for Marta's four hours
   lewis_decision_note.md           one page, six decisions, for Lewis at 10:00 Friday
   amira_event_allowance.md         cost note and allowance for Amira
   operator_review_checklist.md     Sam's one-page checklist
   decision_log_template.csv        the log Sam fills in
   event_runbook.md                 hour-by-hour plan for game night
   plan_and_raci.md                 deadlines, day-by-day plan, RACI

4. ai_log/ai_use_log.md
   Chronological account of how AI was used and checked.

TO RUN THE DEMONSTRATION (Python 3.9 or later, no dependencies)

   python3 -I checks/reconcile.py data outputs
   python3 -I checks/numbers.py
   python3 -I checks/test_reconcile.py
   python3 -I prompts/compare_runs.py

   Full steps and what each shows: README.md section 4.

WHAT THE OTHER FOLDERS HOLD

   data/        the evidence pack as supplied, unchanged

   checks/      the scripts and tests
   prompts/     current and revised AI instructions, and the runner
   outputs/     everything the scripts produce, including saved prompt runs
