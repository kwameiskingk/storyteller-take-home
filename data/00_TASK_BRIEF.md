# AI and Product Delivery Take Home Task

## The exercise

You have taken over delivery of a new AI-enabled content service for the fictional Aurora Basketball League. A pilot is running, the client has raised concerns, and the service is due to support a Championship Finals game in eight days.

Your outcome is for the client's editors to get useful, trustworthy recommendations during Game 3, with an operating approach the available team can deliver. You own establishing what can realistically be achieved and getting the people, decisions and practical work into place. Your responsibility includes what happens before launch, during the event and in the following weeks.

Use the accompanying evidence to decide what should happen and demonstrate useful progress. We want to understand the decisions you make, the evidence you use and how you turn those decisions into action.

## Your remit

You are the Delivery Lead. You can organise the delivery work, direct the available operator, change draft instructions and prepare or test a temporary workflow. You can recommend changes to launch scope, timing and source coverage.

Engineering owns production code changes. Product and the account lead need to agree any change to the external launch commitment. You are responsible for bringing them a clear recommendation and keeping the work moving.

The evidence pack describes the resources currently available. You may propose alternatives, but do not assume additional people, access, budget or working hours have already been agreed.

## What to submit

Choose the format that best communicates your approach. We need to be able to see:

- Your recommendation about the launch, the evidence behind it, and what would change your decision.
- How you would take the work forward, including what you would personally do and how you would involve others.
- A practical demonstration that advances an important part of the delivery outcome, run against the supplied material, with its output and a way for us to repeat it.
- The first update you would send to the client.
- How you used AI and how you checked its contribution. Include a representative prompt or instruction and output, explain anything you changed or rejected, and identify limitations. There is no need to invent a rejected suggestion if none occurred.

Take one important next step as far as you can with the supplied material. Show what is now completed, what remains unresolved and how that changes what you would do next.

The practical demonstration can use code, a spreadsheet, a repeatable AI workflow or another suitable method. We should be able to follow your steps with a fresh copy of the input and understand what still needs human judgment. A diagram or description alone is not a demonstration.

State assumptions and missing information that affect your decisions. Explain what you can do while those questions remain open. You do not need to solve every issue or build a production system.

## Time and submission

This is a paid exercise. The payment amount and submission date will be confirmed in your invitation.

We expect approximately three to four hours of focused work. This is guidance, not a hard time limit. We suggest allowing several calendar days to fit the exercise around your other commitments.

Tell us approximately how long you spent and what you deliberately left unfinished. We value initiative and care in the finished work. Useful additions, thoughtful presentation and work you would be confident putting in front of a client can strengthen your submission. Use your judgment about where extra effort will make the biggest difference.

AI use is expected. You may use tools available to you; paid tooling and live infrastructure are not required. Do not share credentials or give us access to your personal accounts. If your workflow depends on a tool we may not have, include representative outputs and sufficiently clear steps for us to inspect how it works.

We will discuss your decisions and demonstration in the interview, including how you would adapt when new information arrives.
